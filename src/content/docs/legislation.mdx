## What is here?

The legislation corpus is built from the California Legislature's official public bulk-data archive. Each imported bill is preserved with its bill identifier, session, measure number, status, versions, legislative actions, votes, authors, and analyses.

The current build is designed so additional historical sessions can be imported without changing the site structure.

## Source and provenance

The source archive is published by the California Legislative Counsel Bureau at the state's official legislative-information download service. The archive contains the bill tables and the associated XML/text content used to reconstruct legislative records. urlOfficial California bulk-data directoryhttps://downloads.leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/

The site keeps the official identifiers so records can be traced back to the source data rather than relying on an independently invented numbering scheme.
